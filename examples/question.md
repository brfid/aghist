# Technology and work

This sample illustrates a question file. It is a starting formulation for the documentation examples, not a finding or an adopted question from an existing research library.

## Question

Under what conditions, and through what mechanisms, do changes in work practices help people obtain benefits from a newly introduced technology?

## Definitions and scope

- **Newly introduced technology:** new to the people or organization using it; record the setting and period.
- **Work practices:** tasks, responsibilities, skills, or coordination arrangements. Identify the actual practice rather than assuming a formal reorganization.
- **Benefits:** specify the observed or reported outcome, such as reliability, quality, material use, or coordination. Preserve whose benefit it was and how it was established.
- **Case:** a bounded historical episode or comparison with identifiable actors and a sequence of adoption and use.

## Component claims and assessment criteria

Assess whether a work change contributed to an outcome, through which steps, and what alternative explanation the evidence permits. Keep a helpful contribution separate from a claim that the change was necessary or sufficient.

Seek benefits without new work changes, failure despite work changes, adverse outcomes, and incomplete accounts as well as supporting cases. Separate source observations, attributed explanations, and evaluator inferences. Missing evidence is not evidence of absence.

Explain which components the case addresses, the strength and limits of its evidence, and an inquiry that could challenge the explanation. The initial aim is to explain when and how the relationship holds. A selected case collection does not establish how often it holds across all adoptions.
